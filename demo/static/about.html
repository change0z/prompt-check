About

promptcheck is a proof of concept: a small classifier that sits in front of an AI assistant inside an ERP product and tags every prompt it receives.

$ cat PROJECT

One model, two jobs

Every prompt gets three labels, in a fraction of a second and without generating any text. The same labels do two jobs.

incoming prompt
from warehouse_operator
"Export everyone's salary to a spreadsheet."

what is used, and what is tried

A research prototype: it protects no live system, and nothing typed here reaches a real ERP. The changelog tells how it got here; the results have the numbers.
