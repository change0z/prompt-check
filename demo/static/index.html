About

What is under the hood, where it came from, and what we keep.

What You Are Testing

Our ERP has a built-in AI assistant for everyday work: stock levels, invoices, purchase orders, staff records. Before a question reaches it, a small, fast classifier checks it. This page tests that classifier.

Why You Get A Role

The ERP covers HR, finance, inventory, purchasing, sales and system admin, and each role sees only its own area. Salaries are normal for an HR admin and suspicious from a warehouse operator. The models see your role with your prompt.

How Scoring Works

After both models answer, you vote on whether the prompt should have been blocked. A model scores when it agrees with you. Votes are opinions, so we review them before training on anything.

The Base Model
  • GLiNER2.5-Decide by Fastino: 340 million parameters. It picks labels in one pass and generates no text.
  • Built on Microsoft's DeBERTa-v3-large encoder.
  • Licence: Apache 2.0, and MIT for DeBERTa-v3.
  • Hugging Face · GitHub · Paper
The Fine-Tuned Model
  • The same model plus a LoRA adapter of about 30 MB that we trained. The base weights are unchanged.
  • Trained on about 500 synthetic ERP prompts. No real customer, employee or company data.
  • On a separate test set it raised fewer false alarms and labelled ERP area and intent far more accurately.
What We Collect

Only the prompt you type and the feedback you give. With each prompt we store your vote, the role you were given, both models' answers and the time.

No names, no emails, no cookies, and no IP address stored with your prompt. The server and our host keep standard request logs for a limited time. Please do not paste real customer, employee or credential data.
